Flock Calendar

A care schedule for layer farms that tells you what each batch of birds needs and when — so you never hold the whole routine in your head.

Status: approved Markdown is canonical · this page is a companion

The kernel

A schedule generator for recurring flock care. Instead of the farmer entering reminders, the app lays out every recurring task — vaccines, deworming, cage cleaning, water changes — per batch, driven by bird age and interval, and shows what's due this week. The pain is “not knowing when,” not “forgetting,” so the app generates the schedule rather than just reminding. Livestock health schedulers are an underserved niche; farm finance tools are not.

How it fits together

The schedule generates itself from bird age and interval — so the app holds the routine, not the farmer.

Who it's for

Monday Samuel Chidera runs a layer farm of 80+ birds in multiple batches at different ages (some around one year, some around two). Today he tracks care in memory and by marking calendars, and his pain is “not knowing when to clean up” — he has other things going on in life. The app also serves other small poultry farmers managing more than one batch.

What “working” looks like

The moment

Monday opens the app and sees, per batch, what's due this week — e.g. “Batch A: deworming due · Batch B: cage cleaning due · water change daily” — and checks each off as he does it.

The “oh, that's cool” beat

He doesn't have to remember anything. The app tells him what the flock needs, and it's right.

✕ Explicitly cut click to expand
  • Money / profit tracking — real and important, but a second kernel; deferred so the care kernel stays sharp.
  • Other animal types — kept out so the demo stays sharp and buildable; the schedule should be data so this is cheap later.
  • Notifications — later; the PoC proves the schedule, not delivery.
  • Deployment — optional; a demo video and public repo are what's required.
